Burning Cole

They thought he was finished.

Cole Sinclair runs Club 108, a luxury West End nightclub. He also has a boss with two wives, a feisty assistant manager, a gangster on speed dial, one real friend and court-ordered therapy. And he's telling you all about it.

Format8 × 50–60 min · season one
PilotGood Morning Mr Sinclair (scripted)
SettingClub 108, London's West End
Created byObi Offomata & Camilo Parra-Braun

The world

Burning Cole is set in London nightlife. Cole Sinclair manages Club 108, a dark-marble-and-brass West End club where the job is simple: fill it with pretty people who like to spend money. Everything else in his life is less simple.

His boss Mo owns the club and has a marriage that is about to get complicated. Anna, his assistant manager, runs rings around him. London, a gangster with impeccable manners, is the man Cole calls when he needs something sorted. His best mate TC doubles as his bodyguard. And a judge has made sure that every week ends in a chair opposite Dr Stoker.

How it plays

Cole narrates his own life straight to the audience, part confession and part "how to" guide for men on dating, playing the field and the situations to avoid. He is dry, cheeky and usually a step behind the chaos he has caused. The club is the serious side of the show, with the real pressures of running a venue. The people in it are the comedy. And underneath it all, a figure from Cole's past is coming back.

Where it comes from

The series grew out of Obi's blog about years spent working in London nightclubs and bars: the dates, the rejections and everything that happens behind the velvet rope. Recognised UK artists feature throughout the series, putting the British music scene on screen where it belongs.

Genre

British dark comedy

Think

Sex and the City meets Entourage, in a London nightclub.

Audience

18–40, anyone who has ever had a big night out and wondered what goes on behind the bar.

Available

Ikenga

250 years of service. One way out.

Behind the glass towers of the City, a secret syndicate of London law firms settles what the courts can't. Arinze Ezenwa, known at work as Morgan Hale, is one of the people they send.

Format8 × 50 min · extended 70 min pilot
The firmHale, Ashcombe and Thorne
StatusPilot in writing
Created byObi Offomata
Main theme Ikenga
0:00 / 2:39

The title

In Igbo tradition, the ikenga is the shrine of a man's right hand: his strength, his drive and what he achieves by his own effort. The series asks what that strength costs when it's put to work in the shadows.

The world

By day, Arinze is a star litigator at Hale, Ashcombe and Thorne, winning civil fraud cases in court. By night, the firm uses him for the cases that can't go to court: finding people who have disappeared, recovering what was stolen and protecting clients from rivals who don't play by the rules. A rival firm wants the same clients, and it has its own people.

The season opens seven months ahead, with Arinze pinned down and shot in a farmyard, then goes back to show how he got there.

If I open the door, others can come through.

In the summer of 2020, during lockdown, after the killing of George Floyd, the streets of London filled with people asking for change and for accountability. Obi Offomata watched and asked himself what he could actually do. He wasn't a politician, an entertainment mogul or an influencer. What he had was his writing.

Obi grew up in the inner city of Southeast London, in a working-class family that came here from Nigeria. Like the people who came before him, he wanted to make a place in the world for himself, and to make room for others while he did it.

Make the ideas so loud that the networks have to listen.

A collective, not a company of one

Urban Shakespeare brings together writers with stories that haven't been seen, along with the people who can help bring them to the screen. Alone, a writer without a network or funding hits a wall. Together, we're harder to ignore.

Why AI

For years the barrier was money. You needed finance to shoot a pilot and a network to get it seen. In 2026 that has changed. We write the scripts and episodes, then use AI to produce the trailers, posters and artwork that show what each series will look like. Every project on this site is a proof that it can be done, and an invitation to make it for real.

Founder · Obi (Alex) Offomata
